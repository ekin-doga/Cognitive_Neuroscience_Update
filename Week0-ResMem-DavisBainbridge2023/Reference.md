## Data and credits

The paintings are public-domain images from the [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images), released under a CC0 Public Domain Designation, and resized for this course.

The human memorability scores and the authors' ResMem predictions are a subset of the behavioral data from:

> Davis, T., & Bainbridge, W. A. (2023, June 19). *Memorability of Art* [Data set]. OSF. https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/VHP5D

Please cite the associated paper if you use these data:

> Davis, T. M., & Bainbridge, W. A. (2023). Memory for artwork is predictable. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 120*(28), e2302389120.
